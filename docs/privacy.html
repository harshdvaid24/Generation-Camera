Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

This policy covers Generation Camera, the camera app for Android (package name com.generationcamera). It is an independent, open-source project.

The short version

The app collects nothing.

Generation Camera does not collect, store on servers, transmit or share any data. It cannot: the app does not declare Android's internet permission, so Android gives it no network access at all.

Everything happens on your device, offline. Your photos are saved to your own photo storage and stay there unless you choose to share them.

What the app can access

The app uses two Android permissions.

Camera android.permission.CAMERA
Used to show the live viewfinder and to take photos. The camera image is processed on your device. A photo is saved only when you take one.
Storage, on Android 9 and older only android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
On Android 9 and older, an app needs this permission to save photos into the Pictures folder. The app uses it to save its own photos and to show them in its gallery, and for nothing else. On Android 10 and later the app has no storage permission at all.

The app does not request the internet permission, location, the microphone or your contacts.

A technical footnote: the installed app also lists com.generationcamera.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. A standard Android support library adds it automatically. It is internal to the app and gives it no access to your data or to any device feature.

What the app collects

Nothing.

  • No account, sign-in or profile.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics and no trackers.
  • No third-party data SDKs.
  • No location, no microphone, no video recording.

The app keeps no personal data of its own. The only things it creates are the photos you take, and those go straight into your own photo storage.

Where your photos live, and how to delete them

Photos are saved as JPEG files in Pictures/GenerationCamera, in your device's shared photo storage. They are ordinary files, and they are yours.

Because that is your normal photo storage, your gallery app, your file manager and any other app you have allowed to see your photos can see them too. If you use a backup or sync app that covers this folder, it copies the photos under its own settings and its own privacy policy. Generation Camera has no part in that.

To delete a photo, open your gallery or file manager app, go to the GenerationCamera album (the Pictures/GenerationCamera folder) and delete it there. To remove all of them, delete the folder.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your photos. They stay on your device until you delete them.

The app holds nothing anywhere else, so there is no other copy to request, export or erase.

What is inside a photo file

The app never accesses your location, so your photos carry no GPS tags.

The only EXIF fields the app adds to a photo are:

  • UserComment: the decade and degree you shot with, for example GenerationCamera era=1970s degree=7.
  • Software: Generation Camera.

The file name contains the decade and the time the photo was taken.

A few things are drawn into the picture itself, where anyone who sees the photo can read them: a small credit line with the decade and a camera name, the date stamp if you switch it on (1990s and 2000s looks), and the note you type on an instant print.

When a photo leaves the app

Only when you tap Share. Android then shows its share sheet, and you pick where the photo goes.

From that point the photo is handled by the app you picked, under that app's privacy policy. What gets shared is the same JPEG file, including the metadata described above.

Children

Generation Camera is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

This website

This website is two static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no scripts and has no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical details such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages, under its own privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. The page is kept in the app's public source repository, so earlier versions stay visible there.

Contact
