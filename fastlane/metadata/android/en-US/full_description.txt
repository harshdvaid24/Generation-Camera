Most cameras shoot the present. This one has a dial for the past.

Generation Camera has a rotary decade dial. Turn it, and the live viewfinder and the saved photo take on the look of that decade's everyday photo medium. Sepia dry plates. 8 mm home movies. Camcorder tape. Then today, as it is.

The dial: 14 stops
- 1900s: sepia dry plate. Reds go dark, skies blow out, heavy oval vignette, soft glow, dust.
- 1910s: Autochrome. Pastel color with starch-grain speckle.
- 1920s: early 35 mm black and white.
- 1930s: pale, grainy newsreel black and white.
- 1940s: punchy press black and white.
- 1950s: warm, saturated slide color.
- 1960s: 8 mm home-movie film. Warm, vignetted, with gate weave and flicker in the live preview.
- 1970s: faded print with light leaks.
- 1980s: vibrant 35 mm color negative.
- 1990s: camcorder tape. Scanlines, chroma bleed, tape flutter.
- 2000s: early digicam. Oversharpened, cool flash white balance, noise.
- 2010s: early smartphone with mild HDR.
- 2020s: clean computational look.
- 2026: today, as it is.

Degree: a hint or the full treatment
- A slider from 0 to 10. 0 is subtle, 10 is the maximum.
- Grain, dust and light leaks ramp up late, so low degrees stay clean.

The controls time-travel too
- Flash and self-timer (3 or 10 seconds) from the 1930s on.
- Zoom from the 1960s on.
- Optional date stamps: date and time in the 1990s, orange date in the 2000s.
- A rule-of-thirds grid from the 2010s on.
- Exposure (EV) and the front or back camera in every decade.
- Synthesized shutter sounds change with the era, from a plate-camera clack to a digicam beep.

Instant prints with a handwritten note
- Flip the Frame switch and the shot ejects and develops as an instant print: a square photo on a white card.
- Type a one-line note before you save. It lands on the card in handwriting.
- The card is an exact 4:5 canvas, ready for social posts.

What you see is what you save
- Stills run through the same real-time GPU shaders as the viewfinder.
- Photos save as JPEG to Pictures/GenerationCamera on your device.
- Swipe through them in the gallery. Share with the system share sheet.
- Photos only. No video.

Offline and private
- Everything runs on your device.
- The app never requests the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- No account. No ads. No analytics. No trackers.
- No location access, no microphone, no GPS tags in your photos.
